Welcome to RoboTac 2027

Touch provides robots with direct access to physical interaction, revealing contact, force, friction, slip, compliance, deformation, and object dynamics that cannot always be inferred from vision alone.

As robotics moves toward Embodied AI and Physical AI, RoboTac 2027 focuses on tactile world models that help robots predict and control interactions for robust grasping and manipulation.
