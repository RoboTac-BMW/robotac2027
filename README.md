Workshop Porposal:
Title: RoboTac 2027: Benchmarking Tactile Intelligence for Grasping & Manipulation: Emerging Tactile World Models for Embodied Robotics

Abstract: Touch provides robots with direct access to physical interaction, revealing contact, force, friction, slip, compliance, deformation, and object dynamics that cannot always be inferred from vision alone. As robotics moves toward Embodied AI and Physical AI, an emerging challenge is to develop Tactile World Models that enable robots not only to perceive contact, but to learn the dynamics of physical interaction, predict the consequences of their actions, and use these predictions for adaptive grasping and manipulation.

The RoboTac workshop series has evolved from tactile perception and learning, through visuo-tactile perception, predictive coding and active inference, and data-driven learning and control, toward embodied tactile intelligence and multimodal foundation models. The 9th series of RoboTac WS (RoboTac 2027) takes the next step by placing Tactile World Models at the center of the workshop and, for the first time, evaluating emerging approaches through a coordinated international sim-to-real benchmark.

The workshop will bring together leading researchers in world models, tactile sensing and learning, robot manipulation, simulation, and embodied AI through invited keynotes, contributed papers, posters, demonstrations, panels, and a benchmark challenge.

Approximately three months before ICRA 2027, RoboTac, together with Daimon Robotics, plans to distribute 20 DM-DataClaw and DM-TacClaw hardware sets to 20 selected international laboratories. DM-DataClaw enables multimodal tactile demonstration and data collection, while DM-TacClaw provides the corresponding actuated tactile robotic platform for real-world execution and evaluation. In parallel, a corresponding simulation environment will be made available to the broader community.

Teams will investigate how tactile and multimodal world models can learn interaction dynamics, predict future contact and physical states, anticipate grasp stability and object motion, and support planning and control. Approaches will be evaluated on common grasping and manipulation tasks, with simulation, real-hardware, generalization, and sim-to-real results presented at RoboTac 2027.

By combining Tactile World Models with shared simulation, common physical hardware, standardized tasks, and international comparative evaluation, RoboTac 2027 aims to advance the transition from tactile perception to predictive physical intelligence.
